## Example Feature Project: Completion Summary

### 1. Project story
The 'Example Feature Project' focused on the rapid development and refinement of a dashboard prototype designed to provide a product-minded overview of project status. We initiated the project with a basic screen mock and iteratively built towards a comprehensive visual interface. This involved deliberate effort in defining visual hierarchy, optimizing layout density, and ensuring the display was intuitive enough for stakeholders to grasp project status at a glance.

### 2. Key achievements
*   **Functional Prototype Delivered:** Successfully built and refined a working dashboard prototype, achieving the goal of presenting project status effectively.
*   **Enhanced User Experience:** Significantly improved visual hierarchy, layout density, and mobile responsiveness, addressing initial design challenges.
*   **Efficient Execution:** Consistently completed engineering sessions ahead of schedule (e.g., 45 mins vs. 60 mins planned; 40 mins vs. 45 mins planned), demonstrating agile and focused development.
*   **Clear Communication:** Created a documented feature that succinctly captures the design and engineering efforts for quick review by both technical and non-technical audiences.

### 3. Lessons learned
*   **AI Augmentation Limitations:** Initial AI-generated designs often miss critical human-centric details like nuanced mobile spacing, optimal card density, and the full scope of visual hierarchy required for a polished product.
*   **Value of Iterative Design:** The most impactful refinements came from a trial-and-error process and collaborative debates over layout, underscoring that engineering effort extends beyond initial coding to deep design iteration.
*   **Prioritizing Clarity:** For dashboards, ensuring information is digestible "at a glance" requires meticulous attention to hierarchy and density, which were key areas of improvement during refinement.

### 4. Next steps
With the robust dashboard prototype successfully completed, the immediate next steps involve gathering comprehensive feedback from stakeholders and conducting user testing. This will validate the current design decisions and inform the subsequent iterations required to evolve the prototype into a production-ready feature, potentially integrating it into broader project management tools.

### 5. Proof points
*   **Visual Prototype:** The "Dashboard prototype" screenshot demonstrates the refined user interface and achieved visual clarity.
*   **"Status at a Glance" Achieved:** As noted in the logs, "A simple layout exists and stakeholders can understand the status at a glance."
*   **Design Iteration & Refinement:** Log entries detail the process of how "cleaned up the hierarchy, tightened the layout" after initial challenges.
*   **Development Efficiency:** Documented session durations confirm completion under planned times, highlighting focused and effective work.
*   **Strategic Documentation:** The feature was "documented... to be reviewed quickly by hiring managers or teammates," indicating foresight in showcasing the work.
